# Fonts

All three families are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (see OFL.txt).

- Instrument Serif, Rodrigo Fuenzalida and Jordan Egstad, https://github.com/Instrument/instrument-serif
- Geist and Geist Mono, Vercel, https://github.com/vercel/geist-font

The TTF files here are the static instances served by Google Fonts. They are used only at build time
by `scripts/build-assets.mjs` and `scripts/activity.mjs` to outline text into SVG paths.
